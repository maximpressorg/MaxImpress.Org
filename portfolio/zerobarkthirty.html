Situation & objective

The account arrived with a small audience, no monetization and a broad review format that required better publishing structure to grow. The first objective was to qualify for the then-current creator revenue program; the second was to turn that qualification push into sustainable audience development.

What the engagement involved

Creator Revenue MonetizationAccount GrowthPublishing StrategyConnection Development

Our approach

  • Built the initial campaign around the five-million-impression monetization threshold and completed the qualification phase within the standard 10–12 day operating window.
  • After monetization, shifted from threshold-driven activity into broader account growth, stronger publishing strategy and connection development.
  • Used the reviewer identity as the account anchor so growth could support multiple review topics without making the profile feel directionless.
