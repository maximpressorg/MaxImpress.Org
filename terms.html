SERVICE INFORMATION

Clear work.
Clear terms.

These general guidelines explain our public service boundaries. Your individual written agreement governs paid engagements.

What is agreed before payment

Each project needs a documented service scope, channels, deliverables, timelines, revision approach, client responsibilities, pricing, third-party costs, payment arrangements and escalation contact.

Results and outside platforms

Audience growth, advertising results, search rankings, platform approvals, monetization payouts and algorithmic reach are influenced by third parties and are not guaranteed. Where older service descriptions refer to follower or view figures, the actual meaning and any conditions must be defined in the final written agreement.

Cancellation, refunds and disputes

Applicable notice periods, renewals, refunds, disputes and work-completion conditions must be included in your final written agreement before payment. This informational page is not a promise of universal refunds, escrow or a particular response-time guarantee.

Ownership and licensed assets

Clients retain ownership and control of their original social accounts, domains and hosting. Ownership of produced content, editable sources, design assets, licensed music, stock footage and third-party materials should be expressly stated in the project agreement.

Account access

Use official delegated business permissions where available and avoid sending account passwords by email or web forms. The client should be able to review and revoke granted roles.

Other costs

Paid advertising spend, domains, hosting, premium tools and paid external subscriptions are outside ordinary management prices unless an individual quotation explicitly includes them.

Questions?

Before paying, ask maximpress.org@gmail.com for the current terms that apply to your exact service.
