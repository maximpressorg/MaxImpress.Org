PRIVACY & DATA

Your information
deserves care.

Plain-English information about the static inquiry experience and the information you choose to send.

Information in the contact form

The current static preview processes your inquiry within your browser. Clicking Prepare opens an email draft addressed to MaxImpress; the site does not transmit that inquiry to a MaxImpress database automatically. Submitting the email is your decision.

Information you email

If you send an inquiry, your email provider and recipient email service process the contents under their own privacy terms. Send only information necessary to discuss your project. Do not include passwords, bank details or identity documents in ordinary communications.

Browser storage

The introduction uses session storage solely to avoid replaying the entry animation on every page in the same browser session. No analytics SDK or advertising tracker is bundled into this preview.

External links

Choosing X, Instagram or email opens a third-party service that applies its own terms. External platform links do not mean MaxImpress controls that platform.

Account permissions

Any future account access should be agreed separately and, where possible, authorized through official platform business permissions. Clients can manage those roles in the platform settings.

Updates and rights

Hosting analytics, backend features or embedded services may require updated disclosures before production launch. To ask about any information sent by email, contact maximpress.org@gmail.com.
