QUESTIONS, ANSWERED

The practical details matter.

Clear answers before you make a decision.

Are advertising costs included?+

No. Advertiser media spend is paid by you to the platform, separately from the management fee.

How many social platforms are included?+

Brand Social Management covers up to two social platforms. The higher-tier plan is multi-platform; its precise channels are agreed in writing.

Can we request content revisions?+

Yes, feedback and approval arrangements are part of scope confirmation. Specific revision limits and delivery timings are agreed before work starts.
