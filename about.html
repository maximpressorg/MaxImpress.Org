ABOUT MAXIMPRESS

About MaxImpress.

MaxImpress is a digital growth company based in New York, USA serving creators, brands, businesses and organizations. Our work centers on content strategy, platform management, publishing coordination, audience development and campaign support. From the first conversation, we aim to define the scope, establish a clear approval process and connect clients with the people responsible for delivery. We work across different industries and social platforms while keeping account ownership and commercial expectations transparent.
