HOW WE WORK

Good work needs a clear process.

Our process is built to keep workflow, trial expectations, approvals, payment routes and account safety visible from the start.

PROCESS & INSIGHTS

A clearer view of how MaxImpress works.

The structure below keeps the process direct: what happens first, how the free trial works, how execution begins, and how payments and platform access are handled.

01

Discovery & direction

We review the client goal, platform mix, content needs, and the most suitable service path before anything moves forward.

  • Define the main objective
  • Confirm the platform scope
  • Recommend the right service package
02

4–6 day free trial

For Creator, Organization, and Brand Social Media Management, MaxImpress may offer a 4–6 day free trial to show the practical workflow before payment.

  • Scheduling preview
  • Content handling preview
  • Draft posting and coordination flow
03

Approval, access & setup

We confirm deliverables, timelines, permissions, and the safest workable access method before active management begins.

  • Role-based access where possible
  • Delegation features when supported
  • Written scope before execution
04

Execution & check-ins

Once approved, we move into active handling: publishing, content coordination, optimization and service follow-through.

  • Platform-specific execution
  • Revision and review checkpoints
  • Clear communication throughout
05

Reporting, renewal & payment continuity

After work is underway or a cycle is completed, we review progress, next-step options, and the agreed payment route for continued work.

  • Visible payment route
  • Renewal only when agreed
  • Follow-up and next-step planning
FREE TRIAL EXPLAINED

The trial is about workflow visibility — not exaggerated promises.

During the 4–6 day free trial, we do not promise guaranteed growth results. Instead, we show the client how the workflow looks in practice.

  • Scheduling and posting rhythm
  • Content handling and coordination
  • Drafting, formatting and review checkpoints
  • How communication and approvals would work in a full engagement
ACCOUNT SAFETY

Normal work does not require your official password.

Under normal conditions, MaxImpress does not ask for official account passwords. Where possible, work is handled through platform-supported delegation, business permissions, team access or role-based access tools.

  • X can be handled via delegation
  • Instagram and Facebook can be handled via Meta permissions
  • YouTube and TikTok follow the platform’s available team or access methods
PAYMENT OPTIONS

Clear payment instructions.
Recognizable international providers.

Subject to the invoice and account details issued for your specific project, the following methods may be offered. We confirm currency, fees and payment instructions in writing before funds are sent.

PayPal

Familiar international checkout with clear payment records and wide client familiarity for cross-border transactions.

Official details are included in your invoice when available.

Stripe

Invoice-based card payments through an eligible Stripe-hosted checkout or verified payment link where available.

Check the recipient name and amount before paying.

Payoneer

International business payment requests and multi-currency arrangements, subject to receiving-account eligibility.

Use the payment request officially issued to you.

Crypto · USDT preferred

USDT may be accepted by arrangement. If you hold another supported asset, we can confirm the correct chain, token details, and secure payment route first.

Blockchain transfers can be irreversible, so wallet address and network details must be verified carefully.

The appearance of a provider logo does not certify MaxImpress as that provider’s partner. Payment availability must be confirmed in the project’s written invoice before funds are transferred.
