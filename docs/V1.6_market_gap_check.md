# MaxImpress V1.6 — Service and Trust Feature Audit

Comparison baseline: prior screenshots covering BELAY, GKV, $99 Social, Contra and Toptal. This audit tracks actual website content, not unverified market superiority.

| Feature observed in competitor research | V1.6 status | Important boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Productized service catalog and transparent prices | Present: 19 existing package entries, previous prices preserved | Some plan scopes are tailored; negotiate in writing. |
| Step-by-step intake and project workflow | Present: six-stage flowchart and detailed process | Client-specific delivery agreement needed. |
| Content approvals and revisions | Described, content approval guide and contract language | No live approval portal; number and timing of revisions are not universal. |
| Reporting / analytics | Explained by package and reporting guide | Actual sample reports and verified outcomes not yet provided. |
| Account permissions and client control | Permission-based guidance and clear ownership of accounts | No technical certification or integrated access-audit portal. |
| Transparent monthly spend | Paid-ad costs, software/domain/hosting exclusions described | No universal ad-management rate independent of selected plans. |
| Refunds, disputes, contract terms | Terms and process FAQs describe that final agreement controls | No invented money-back guarantee or platform escrow. |
| Client portfolio | 26 prior case summaries, 17 user-supplied screenshots, 26 old circular client images | Six uploads remain unmatched to identities; performance metrics are not audited. |
| Independent reviews / Google or Trustpilot source verification | Dedicated Reviews page and review standards | Genuine sourced testimonials/ratings still needed; do not fabricate. |
| Staff and leader identities / credentials | Leadership-card structure ready | Real names, biographies, awards and performance proof still needed. |
| Direct contact and booking | Animated compact Contact Us panel; request meeting + Gmail + X + Instagram | No live booking calendar/Zoom URL; WhatsApp temporarily unavailable. |
| Ownership of editable creative sources | Scope/terms clarification present | Must explicitly agree file delivery, copyright and third-party rights per client. |
| Organization, creators and brands segments | Separate service pages and packages | Specific platforms vary by selected tier. |
| Standalone lead-capture automation | Not offered as a standalone verified product | Should not be advertised without an actual implemented service. |

**Summary:** Many of the information and expectation gaps identified in the earlier five-site research are addressed as disclosures or planned operating processes. Third-party reviews, confirmed leadership credentials, a secure client portal, escrow, and demonstrated real case outcomes cannot be truthfully simulated through website design.
