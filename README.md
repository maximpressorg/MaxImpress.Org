# MaxImpress — Completely New Website V1.5 (Review Draft)

This is a **separate rebuild** of MaxImpress, derived from new-site V1.3—not an edit to the original official 11.13 website. Extract and open `index.html` in Chrome, Edge or Safari. Network access is required to load the optional decorative Unsplash/Pexels photo backgrounds on the Services catalog; bundled SVG fallback artwork is available offline.

## Requested V1.5 changes

- Direct homepage start: intro/splash/loading and Skip Introduction deleted from all HTML and JS.
- Original MaxImpress logo maintained across navigation and pages.
- Top-right CTA restored to **Start Your Project**; nonintrusive floating **Contact Us** shortcut below the initial homepage hero.
- Calmer indigo/blue/teal palette; consistent button borders; light-only theme.
- Hero platform icons include Meta; standalone branding/footer line returned to normal page flow; decorative badges removed; compact mobile workflow panel prevents overlap.
- Services catalog: taller single-color creator, organization, brand, platform, website and intelligence cards; distinct creator/brand colors; abstract brand-mark motif is **decorative only, not claimed client logos**.
- Optional decorative third-party free-stock photograph URL backgrounds for creator, organizations and web development; fallback vector graphics are bundled.
- Plan cards have varied branded background tones, hover movement and animated full-card expansion. **All 19 original priced packages**, their names and amounts preserved exactly.
- Process opening made compact; extra helpful information on deliverables, approvals, permissions and reporting added.
- About page has United States flag, company-provided 10+ years and 50-person team statements, full company introduction, original MP4 beside introduction with yellow trim, and three expandable/linked **Team Leadership** profile pages. No fictional identities, award counts, client ratings or verification badges.
- Video playback: browser policies usually prohibit autoplay with sound; script tries unmuted autoplay and falls back to muted autoplay with **Play with sound** button if blocked. Users can play from the actual video controls.

## Important notes

1. Claims such as United States base, team size and ten-year experience come from the business owner and require owner verification prior to launch.
2. Individual leadership credentials, independent reviews, awards, and team performance claims have not been invented. Profile fields await authentic source records.
3. The service catalog's use of public third-party photos is decorative and does **not** imply an affiliation with people depicted or an endorsement from other brands.
4. Contact intake still opens a user-reviewed email draft; it is not a connected server-side submission or payment processor.
5. Logo and 26 original client-portfolio records retained. Validate all public case claims before publishing.
6. Header navigation: Home, Services, Work & Reviews, Process & Insights, About Us. Floating Contact Us and Start Your Project lead to inquiry flow.
